Hardware

Speaker
Battery backup
The speaker is a MAX98357A on GP18/19/21 (GP16 switches it off when quiet); battery backup reads the PowerBoost on GP26 to GP28. Turn battery backup on only once it is wired: unconnected, those pins read nonsense.

Mirror image
Status banner
Partial updates (the clock, menus) wear the picture a little each time on this panel. A full clean refresh runs 15 s after you stop using it, and this often while it sits.
The controller picks its e-ink waveform by temperature, but its sensor is on the driver board, not the glass, and reads warm after a lot of refreshing, which fades the picture. This forces the value. Lower is darker; keep it within a few degrees of the room.

Home Assistant

MQTT
Topics
With the MQTT integration in Home Assistant, the panel appears as one device with every control here: sleep sounds, rain and thunder, the dawn chorus, alarms, lights and A/C, night mode, the dashboard, and an Events entity for automations.

Wi-Fi

Reset

Erases every setting on the panel: alarms, sounds, lights, Home Assistant, the live dashboard, pairing. It then restarts with the Wi-Fi and server built into its firmware and pairs itself again.
