Hardware

  
  

    
Speaker

    
Battery backup

    
The speaker is a MAX98357A on GP18/19/21 (GP16 switches it off when quiet); battery backup reads the PowerBoost on GP26 to GP28. Turn battery backup on only once it is wired: unconnected, those pins read nonsense.

  


  
Files

  
  

    

    
…

    

    
    

    
Convert sounds for the panelMP3, WAV, M4A and the like become 22 kHz mono ADPCM (about 0.66 MB a minute), levelled, with the end blended into the start so it loops without a seam

    
Files live in the flash after the firmware, so updates leave them alone. While a file is written the sound may stutter.

  


  
Panel

  
  

    
Mirror image

    
Status banner

    
    
    
    
Partial updates (the clock, menus) wear the picture a little each time on this panel. A full clean refresh runs 15 s after you stop using it (unless that is off), and this often while it sits.

    
    
The controller picks its e-ink waveform by temperature, but its sensor is on the driver board, not the glass, and reads warm after a lot of refreshing, which fades the picture. This forces the value. Lower is darker; keep it within a few degrees of the room.

    
    
Black-and-white menu steps sent as one bit per pixel: a quarter of the bytes, so a step streams four times faster. Needs the controller's bitmap mode: try it and look at the glass. If the menus come out white on black, pick the inverted option; if they come out as noise, turn it off.

    

      
      
      
      
    

  


  
Reset

  
  

    
Erases every setting on the panel: alarms, sounds, lights, Home Assistant, the live dashboard, pairing. It then restarts with the Wi-Fi and server built into its firmware and pairs itself again.
