The parts

Reading the view

  • The square is a 256 x 256 mm bed with a 10 mm grid. The two largest parts are 244 mm wide, so they need a bed of at least 250 mm.
  • Orange marks every surface that faces down at more than 45 degrees from vertical and is not sitting on the bed. Those are the places a slicer would put supports.
  • The overhang figure on each card is the area of those orange surfaces, measured from the model. Holes that are bridged, such as screw recesses, count towards it even though they usually print without support.
  • The layer slider cuts the part at that height; press play to watch it build.
